Idle Outpost is a mobile game by Rockbite Games set around building and expanding a post-apocalyptic trading post. The game received wide recognition with almost 50 million downloads on stores and created a series of apocalypse-themed games with similar mechanics. At Rockbite Games I was a part of Idle Outpost Client Team, focused on feature development, and later was working on Performance and Production Maintenance.

## Highlights

- Mobile game project with a large production scope.
- Built with a custom in-house scene and particle editors.
- Complex widgets and custom Backend-Driven approach for layouts.
- Post-apocalyptic outpost progression with trading, upgrades, resources, and new locations.
- Gameplay systems around zombie defense, PvP and PvE battles, heroes, pets, weapons, gear, and live events.
